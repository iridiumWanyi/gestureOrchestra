🎻 Gesture Orchestra — Synth (Web)

Click Start, then allow camera access.
Right index finger: raise it to the upper line to play the black keys (sharps), lower it to the lower line for the white keys; move sideways to pick the note (C3–C5, left = low, right = high). Left hand openness → volume (fist = silent, spread = full).
Camera needs a secure context: open this file via http://localhost (e.g. python3 -m http.server) — file:// may be blocked by the browser.
